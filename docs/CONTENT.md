# Gingerbread Grove: content list

The expanded, Christmas-heavy scope (Nov 17, 2026). Phases are built in order, and each phase is
built, smoke-tested on both loaders, and committed before the next starts. ✅ = done.

All characters and designs are original or drawn from public-domain folklore (Santa Claus,
Krampus, elves, gingerbread men, nutcracker-style soldiers). No licensed characters, songs or
logos.

## Phase 1: World blocks and festive decor

**Ground:** Frosting, Frosted Gingerbread, Gingerbread, Peppermint Stone, Sugar Sand (falls like sand),
Marshmallow (bouncy, no fall damage).

**Woods**, each with log, wood, stripped log/wood, planks, stairs, slab, fence, gate, door,
trapdoor, button, pressure plate, leaves, sapling:
- **Candy Cane**: striped trunk, colourful Gumdrop Leaves
- **Festive Fir**: a Christmas tree. Its leaves come in plain and lit variants (red, gold, blue
  lights that glow), and a Christmas Star grows on top

**Building sets** (block, stairs, slab, wall): Peppermint Stone, Peppermint Bricks, Icing Bricks,
Gingerbread Bricks, Cookie Tiles. Plus Candy Glass and panes.

**Plants:** Gumdrop Bush (harvest gumdrops), Lollipop Flower, Poinsettia, Holly, Sugar Grass.

**Ores:** Sugar Crystal Ore, Cinnamon Ore, Sugar Crystal Block.

**Decor:** Festive Lights (wall-mounted, glowing), Wreath, Christmas Star (light 15), Gift Boxes in
red, green and blue (open one for random loot; found scattered in the world).

## Phase 2: Items, food, tools, Santa gear

**Food:** Gingerbread Cookie, Sugar Cookie, Candy Cane, Gumdrops, Hot Cocoa (Speed), Eggnog
(Regeneration), Marshmallow → Roasted Marshmallow (campfire), Gingerbread Heart (rare, extra hearts).

**Tools:** Sugar Crystal sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel and hoe (between iron and diamond). The sword is a
Candy Cane Sword.

**Santa Suit armor:** hat, coat, trousers, boots. Immune to freezing in powder snow.

## Phase 3: A multi-biome dimension

- **Gingerbread Grove**: candy-cane forest, gumdrop bushes, gingerbread cliffs
- **Festive Fir Forest**: decorated Christmas trees, lights, presents underneath
- **Frosted Flats**: open frosting plains, gift boxes, snowman camps
- **Peppermint Peaks**: striped mountains with iced summits
- **Cocoa Sea**: hot-chocolate ocean with marshmallow islands

Plus giant candy canes as landmarks, gumdrop boulders, and sugar-crystal ore underground.

## Phase 4: Mobs

| Mob | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Gingerbread Man | Passive, flees, drops cookies; catching one is the game |
| Reindeer | Rideable once tamed with sugar cubes; a bell harness gives a glide |
| Peppermint Pup | Tameable companion |
| Elf | Trader in villages and the workshop |
| Santa Claus | Rare, at Santa's Workshop; gives a daily gift, and a better one if you're "nice" |
| Cookie Soldier | Hostile; nutcracker-style guard at outposts, with a shield |
| Gumdrop Blob | Hostile, bounces, splits into smaller blobs |
| Evil Snowman | Hostile, throws slowing snowballs |
| Krampus | Boss at the Candy Cane Outpost; hunts players who are "naughty" |

## Phase 5: Structures and systems

- Structures: Gingerbread House, Candy Cane Outpost, **Santa's Workshop**, Elf Village, present piles
- **Naughty or Nice**: hurting passive festive mobs makes you naughty, and trading or helping
  makes you nice. Santa's gifts and Krampus's attention depend on it
- **Gift wrapping**: wrap any items in a Gift Box to give to a friend
- **Advent Calendar**: a block that gives one gift per real-world day from Dec 1 to Dec 25
- Cookie Jar storage, a Hot Cocoa cauldron

## Later (v1.1)
