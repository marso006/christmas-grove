# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first public beta) - Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric and NeoForge

The first release of Christmas Grove.

### Added

- **The Christmas Grove dimension**, reached with the Snow Globe. It has five biomes: Gingerbread
  Grove, Festive Fir Forest, Frosted Flats, Peppermint Peaks and Cocoa Sea
- **75 blocks**: Candy Cane and Festive Fir wood sets; peppermint, icing, gingerbread and cookie
  building sets; candy glass, sugar sand, bouncy marshmallow; festive lights, wreaths, Christmas
  stars, gift boxes, an advent calendar and a cookie jar
- **Worldgen**: decorated Christmas trees with lights and stars, giant candy canes, marshmallow
  islands, gumdrop bushes, festive flowers, and sugar-crystal and cinnamon ores
- **Structures**: Gingerbread House, Santa's Workshop, Candy Cane Outpost, Snowman Camp
- **Mobs**: Gingerbread Man, Reindeer (rideable, glides with Sleigh Bells), Peppermint Pup (tameable),
  Elf (trader), Santa Claus (daily presents), Cookie Soldier, Gumdrop Blob, Evil Snowman, and the
  Krampus boss
- **Santa's Sleigh** flies over now and then and drops presents on parachutes. A Letter to Santa
  calls it
- **Naughty or Nice**: your actions decide whether Santa brings presents or coal, and whether
  Krampus comes for you
- **Gift wrapping**: pack items into Gift Boxes and give them to friends
- **Food**: hot cocoa, eggnog, cookies, fruitcake, gumdrops, candy canes, roasted marshmallows,
  and the Gingerbread Heart
- **Gear**: Sugar Crystal tools, the Candy Cane Sword, and the Santa Suit (freeze-proof, with a 3D hat)

### Known limitations

- Sounds reuse vanilla sounds; original audio is planned
- English only for now (Bulgarian is planned)
